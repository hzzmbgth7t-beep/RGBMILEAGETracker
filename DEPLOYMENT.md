# Deployment

**Build:** `v2.1.6l-wc10-f16`  
**Build date:** `08/01/2026`  
**Governance:** `v1.7`

1. Retain `v2.1.6l-wc10-f16.zip`.
2. Upload only the 17 ZIP-root files.
3. Open https://hzzmbgth7t-beep.github.io/RGBMILEAGETracker/?v=216lwc10f16
4. Confirm the rounded dark pill appears in the previously accepted blank-screen position when a waiting worker exists.
5. Confirm it reads `Update / Offline / Service / Worker`.
6. Select the indicator.
7. Confirm the waiting worker activates, RGB Mileage reloads, and the indicator disappears.
8. Reopen Settings → Offline Mode and confirm the offline shell is ready.

Do not remove the Home Screen app or clear Safari website data.
