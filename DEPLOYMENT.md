1. Retain `v2.1.6l-wc10-f15.zip`.
2. Upload only the 17 ZIP-root files.
3. Open https://hzzmbgth7t-beep.github.io/RGBMILEAGETracker/?v=216lwc10f15
4. When a waiting worker exists, confirm the green shield shows:
   `Update / Offline / Service / Worker`.
5. Confirm the shield occupies blank space and does not overlap Home content.
6. Apply the update from Settings → Offline Mode.
7. Confirm the shield disappears after activation.
