# RGB Mileage Tracker Changelog

## Version 1.0

Initial full PWA release.

Included:
- Two-vehicle home screen with circular vehicle cards
- Vehicle profiles with photos, badges, purchase data, and insurance information
- Fuel logging with odometer, miles, gallons, fuel grade, fuel type, station, MPG, and notes
- Last fuel selection remembered per vehicle
- Station dropdown management with edit/delete rules
- Fuel history with edit/delete and automatic recalculation
- Vehicle statistics and reports
- Maintenance logging with categories, cost, odometer, notes, and attachments
- Export, import, backup, and restore
- PWA manifest and service worker
- Versioned README, User Guide, and Change Log
